Optional engines installed on demand, after you accept their licenses:
- XTTS-v2 — Coqui Public Model License (non-commercial).
- Silero TTS / VAD — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (TTS models, non-commercial) / MIT (VAD).
- Piper (MIT) and its voices (each voice under its own license, see the voice card).
- faster-whisper (MIT) with Whisper models (MIT, OpenAI). Microsoft Edge TTS (online service, Microsoft terms).
- Higgs TTS 3 on this PC — the open weights bosonai/higgs-tts-3-4b by Boson AI under the Boson Higgs TTS 3 Research and
  Non-Commercial License (research and non-commercial use; clone only voices you have the right to), run by our own server
  code; its environment uses PyTorch (BSD-3-Clause) and Hugging Face transformers (Apache-2.0: the model's audio codec).
- Qwen3-TTS on this PC — the open weights Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base by the Qwen team (Alibaba Cloud), Apache-2.0,
  run by our own server code; its environment uses PyTorch (BSD-3-Clause), the qwen-tts package (Apache-2.0: the prompt,
  the speaker encoder and the 12 Hz audio codec) and Hugging Face transformers (Apache-2.0).

Voice recordings (in the story content pack, story/voice; built in data/voice) were made with Qwen3-TTS
(Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base, Apache-2.0), run locally on the author's computer. Every voice in them is a designed one
(data/speech/voices/designed): made from a text description of the character with Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign
(Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign, Apache-2.0) - our own characters, and sound-alikes of the game's characters
(similar in age, timbre, accent and attitude, but their own voices). No one's recordings were used to make them.
